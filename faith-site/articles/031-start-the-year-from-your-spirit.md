Have you ever started a new year with resolutions that were really just repairs — attempts to fix from the outside what needed to be lived from the inside? Many believers begin seasons with pressure rather than awareness.

Paul's prayer for Philemon gives a better starting point: "That the communication of thy faith may become **effectual** by the **acknowledging of every good thing which is in you in Christ Jesus**" (Philemon 1:6). Here is the central truth of this teaching: **your faith becomes effective as you acknowledge every good thing that is already in you in Christ.**

## Effectual Faith Begins With Acknowledgement

Paul's word for *acknowledging* is *epignosis* — full, precise, experiential knowledge. It is not a nod of agreement but a recognition deep enough to change conduct. Faith becomes effective, Paul says, when it operates from that kind of recognition.

Notice what is being acknowledged: "every good thing which is in you in Christ Jesus." Not every good thing you hope to produce. Not every good thing you plan to become. Every good thing that is already in you — because you are in Christ.

**The New Creation** — the brand-new spiritual person God made in Christ — is the location of those good things. **Spiritual Understanding** — the God-given capacity to perceive and grasp spiritual truth — is the faculty by which you acknowledge them. And **Communion With the Holy Spirit** — ongoing personal fellowship and partnership with the Spirit — is the atmosphere in which that understanding grows.

Think of a house with a fully furnished room behind a closed door. Everything needed is already inside, but until the door is opened and the room acknowledged as yours, you live as though the house were empty. Acknowledgement opens the door of the room that is already furnished.

### Living From the Inside Out

Scripture consistently locates the source of life within the spirit, not in circumstances. Proverbs 4:23 says, "Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life." Your heart — your spirit — is the source, not the symptom.

That means a new year is not primarily about new external conditions; it is about new internal recognition. When you live from your spirit, you bring your mind, emotions, body, relationships, and work under the influence of what God has placed within you. When you live only from circumstances, you react to whatever is loudest.

The difference is this: The outside-in life asks, "What is happening to me, and how should I feel about it?" The inside-out life asks, "What has God placed in me, and how does that shape my response?" The first is governed by events; the second by revelation.

### Why the Spirit Is the Starting Point

Paul's prayer for Philemon is a small masterpiece on this subject: "the communication of thy faith may become effectual by the acknowledging of every good thing which is in you in Christ Jesus" (Philemon 1:6). Effectual faith, Paul says, runs through acknowledgment. What you do not acknowledge does not operate through you.

The word "acknowledging" carries the sense of recognising something as true and giving it full weight. It is not a technique for generating faith; it is the posture of a person who has stopped disputing the facts about himself in Christ.

**The Life of the Spirit** — the Spirit's own life ordering your thoughts and responses — is where such acknowledgement lives. The flesh can only press its case; the spirit has been given the facts.

Proverbs 4:23 makes the guard rail explicit: "Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life." The issues of life do not flow from your calendar. They flow from your inner men.

### The Word Says the Same Thing From Three Directions

- **Colossians 1:27** — "Christ in you, the hope of glory." Paul locates the hope inside the believer. Glory is not merely awaited from above; it is resident within.
- **Romans 8:16** — "The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God." Your spirit is where the Spirit testifies. Acknowledgement begins with listening there.
- **2 Corinthians 5:17** — "If any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new." The good things in you are not improvements to an old self; they belong to a new creation.

Three witnesses, one direction: Christ within, the Spirit's witness, and a new creation — life flows from the inside out.

## What This Means for You

**1. Start each day with acknowledgement, not assessment.** Before you evaluate yesterday or plan today, name what God has placed in you in Christ: righteousness, peace, joy, authority, love, the Holy Spirit. Assessment follows acknowledgement; it should not replace it.

**2. Set the year's goals from identity, not insecurity.** Goals born of insecurity produce pressure; goals born of identity produce purpose. Ask, "Given who I am in Christ, what should I build this year?" rather than "What must I achieve to feel acceptable?"

**3. Guard your heart as a source, not a symptom.** What you allow in — media, conversation, worry — shapes what flows out. Keep the source clean, and the outflow follows.

**4. Faith and wisdom plan the year together.** Faith acknowledges what is within; wisdom builds realistic rhythms — prayer, Scripture, work, rest, giving — that give the inner life room to express itself. Declaring spiritual reality never excuses a planless year; it gives planning a foundation.

## Putting It to Work This Week

- **Write seven good things that are in you in Christ.** Use Scripture to support each one, then read the list aloud each morning this week.
- **Begin one day with acknowledgement before your phone.** Before messages, news, or tasks, spend five minutes naming what God has placed in you and thanking Him for it.
- **Set one goal from identity.** Choose a single aim for this season and write the sentence: because I am ______ in Christ, I will ______. Let identity shape the plan.
- **Guard one source this week.** Identify one input that drains your spirit, and take a concrete step to limit it while adding one input that strengthens it.
