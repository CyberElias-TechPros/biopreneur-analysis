Genesis 39:21 is one of the most quietly important verses in the Old Testament, and it is set in the least glamorous location imaginable.

"But the LORD was with Joseph, and shewed him mercy, and gave him favour in the sight of the keeper of the prison."

Here is the central truth of this teaching: **favour in Scripture is not a random event but an atmosphere that follows a person — Joseph had it in Potiphar's house, in the prison, and in the palace, which means the climate travelled with him and did not depend on the address.**

## The Word

### Ḥen

And the word is *ḥēn* — grace, favour, the disposition of a person toward you that you did not earn.

It is the same word used of Noah finding grace in the eyes of the Lord (Genesis 6:8).

**Favour is unearned by definition, which is why it cannot be demanded.** So why do we keep trying to earn it?

### Repeated Four Times

And that is not a coincidence. The phrase "the LORD was with Joseph" recurs at every stage: in the house, in the prison, and before Pharaoh.

The narrator is making a theological point, not a biographical one.

**The constant is not the circumstance. The constant is the company.**

### Given, Not Attracted

And the verb is important. God *gave* him favour in the sight of the keeper. The keeper did not warm to Joseph by accident.

Favour is an act of God on the disposition of other people.

**God can turn a stranger's heart without asking your permission.**

## Why It Is a Climate

### Because It Moves

And the first reason is that Joseph's favour changed location three times without changing substance.

A climate is portable. A coincidence is not repeatable.

**The same man was favoured in a mansion and in a cell.** Same man, same God, three addresses.

### Because It Is Constant

And the level was not proportional to his comfort. He was a slave, then a prisoner, then a prime minister — and favoured in all three.

Which means circumstances are a bad instrument for measuring it.

**A climate is not the weather. It can be raining in a favoured life.**

### Because It Is Not Earned

And the fourth detail is the one that keeps favour from becoming a technique. Joseph did nothing to deserve the keeper's goodwill. He was a foreign prisoner with a criminal file.

There is no formula in the text, and that is deliberate.

**A climate you cannot manufacture is a climate you cannot lose by failing to maintain it.**

And Potiphar's house was blessed for Joseph's sake (Genesis 39:5), and so was the prison, and so was Egypt.

Favour is not private. It leaks.

**A favoured person makes the whole building do better.**

### What It Is Not

### Not Luck

And it is not chance. Luck has no character behind it and no continuity across time.

Favour has a Person, and it has a pattern.

**A coincidence is a story you tell afterwards. Favour is a story you are living inside.** Which one have you been telling?

### Not Flattery

And favour is not the same as popularity. Joseph was favoured and hated by his brothers in the same decade.

Some people will like you for reasons God arranged. Others will resent you for the same reasons.

Favour does not exempt you from opposition. It outlasts it.

### Not the Absence of Trouble

And the clearest evidence is that Joseph's favour did not keep him out of the pit, off the caravan, or out of the cell.

The promise was not safety. It was presence.

Favour is not a shield around your circumstances. It is a Hand inside them.

### Cultivating the Climate

### Stay in the Presence

And the first practice is the obvious one, and the hardest: the favour is attached to His company, and His company is a relationship, not a formula.

You cannot schedule a climate. You can live in one.

Where He is, that is where the favour is. Be there.

### Serve Well Where You Are

And the second is Joseph's own method. Everything he touched was done so thoroughly that he was put in charge of it.

He did not wait to be favoured before working well.

Excellence is often the doorway that favour walks through.

### Refuse the Bitterness

And the third is the trap. Joseph had every right to be bitter in the prison, and the record gives no hint of it.

Bitterness is the one thing that reliably spoils a climate.

A resentful person can be in the presence and still miss the favour.

### The Boundary

Two guardrails.

First, this is not a formula for career success. Some of the most favoured people in Scripture died in chains, and the letter to the Hebrews makes a point of it (Hebrews 11:36–38).

Second, this is not a reason to despise ordinary work. The favour was on a slave's duties and a prisoner's chores before it was on a nation's economy.

**The **The Presence of God is the source of the climate; Kingdom Stewardship is the post you are stationed at; and Faith and Wisdom is the pairing that keeps favour from making you careless.

## What This Means for You

1. Read Genesis 39 twice, slowly.

2. Note every place the narrator says the Lord was with him.

3. Name the climate you are currently in.

**4. Do one task with Joseph's thoroughness.**

**5. Keep faith and wisdom together.** Favour opens doors; it does not carry you through them. **Kingdom Stewardship** is the assignment; wisdom is the preparation that makes an open door survivable.

### Putting It to Work This Week

- **Read Genesis 41:37–45.**
- **Read Luke 2:39–52.**
- **Write down three "coincidences" in your life.**
- **Look for the pattern in them.**
- **Thank Him for the climate** rather than the weather.
