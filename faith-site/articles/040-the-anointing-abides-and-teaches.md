Have you ever faced a decision so tangled that no amount of advice sorted it out — and then found clarity in a quiet moment of prayer that no counsellor had provided? That clarity has a name.

First John 2:27 says, "But the **anointing** which ye have received of him **abideth in you**, and ye need not that any man teach you: but as the same anointing **teacheth you of all things**, and is truth, and is no lie." Here is the central truth of this teaching: **the anointing you received abides in you permanently and teaches you all things.**

## An Anointing That Remains

In the Old Testament, anointing oil was poured on prophets, priests, and kings to set them apart and equip them for service. It was external, occasional, and revocable — Saul's anointing departed. John announces something radically different: the anointing now *abides in you*. It is internal, permanent, and personal.

**The Anointing** — the Holy Spirit's empowering presence resting upon and within you for God's purposes — is not a rare visitation but a resident reality. **Revelation Knowledge** — truth made alive to your spirit — is how the anointing teaches. **Spiritual Understanding** — the God-given capacity to perceive and grasp spiritual truth — is the faculty it develops.

Think of a built-in compass rather than a map consulted occasionally. A compass does not tell you every detail of the terrain, but it always points true north, and it works wherever you are. The anointing is that internal compass, and the Word is the terrain map. Together they guide you reliably.

## Teaching That Guards and Confirms

John's context is warning about false teachers who denied that Jesus is the Christ. His comfort is not "you need better teachers" but "you have an anointing that teaches." The indwelling Spirit is the believer's safeguard against deception.

This indwelling Teacher also explains why ordinary believers can discern spiritual error without formal training. The same Spirit who inspired the Scriptures recognises His own voice in them, and He alerts the heart when something taught does not match the truth. That inner alarm is not suspicion or a critical spirit; it is the anointing doing its guarding work, and it deserves to be heeded rather than argued away.

Notice three claims John makes about this teaching. It is *truth* — reliable. It is *no lie* — never deceptive. And it teaches you to *abide in Him* — its aim is always deeper union with Christ, never independence from Him. Any supposed anointing that pulls you away from Jesus, from Scripture, or from humble fellowship is not the anointing John describes. Has any teacher ever spoken impressively and still left your spirit uneasy?

Here is the contrast: the untaught believer drifts with every new voice and every persuasive argument. The anointed believer listens widely but weighs everything against the internal witness and the written Word.

## The Word Says the Same Thing From Three Directions

- **John 14:26** — "But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost... he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance." Jesus promised the same teaching ministry John describes: internal, personal, and reliable.
- **1 Corinthians 2:12** — "Now we have received, not the spirit of the world, but the spirit which is of God; that we might know the things that are freely given to us of God." The Spirit is given so that you might *know* — revelation is the purpose, not a bonus.
- **Psalm 23:3** — "He restoreth my soul: he leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for his name's sake." The Shepherd leads; the sheep follows. Guidance is relational, not merely instructional.

Three witnesses, one Teacher: promised by Jesus, given for knowledge, and leading in right paths.

## What This Means for You

**1. Expect internal teaching.** Approach Scripture expecting the anointing to illuminate it. Read prayerfully, pausing where something quickens, and ask what the Spirit is showing you.

**2. Weigh every voice against the Word and the witness.** Teachers, counsel, prophecy, and impressions are valuable but never final. Test all things; hold fast what agrees with Scripture and with the Spirit's inner witness.

**3. Obey what the anointing shows you.** Teaching that is not obeyed quickly becomes theory. Act on the light you have, and more light follows.

**4. Faith and wisdom listen and verify.** Faith trusts the internal Teacher; wisdom seeks confirmation through Scripture, counsel, and circumstance. Declaring the anointing never means independence from the body of Christ; it means discerning within it.

## Putting It to Work This Week

- **Read 1 John 2:20–27 twice.** Mark every phrase about the anointing — received, abides, teaches, truth, no lie — and thank God for the internal Teacher.
- **Practise listening before reading.** Before opening Scripture each day this week, pause for sixty seconds and ask the Spirit to teach you, then read expectantly.
- **Test one voice this week.** When you encounter a claim or impression, write it down and check it against Scripture and wise counsel before acting.
- **Obey one specific insight.** Take one truth the anointing has highlighted recently and put it into practice with a concrete action today.
