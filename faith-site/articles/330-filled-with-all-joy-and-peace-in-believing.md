Romans 15:13 is a benediction, and like most benedictions it is usually read at the end of something rather than obeyed at the start.

"Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that ye may abound in hope, through the power of the Holy Ghost."

Here is the central truth of this teaching: **Paul's prayer is not for more joy but for a filling of it — and the purpose clause tells you why: joy and peace are not the destination, they are the reservoir that makes a person overflow in hope, which means a joyless believer has nothing to pour out on anybody.**

## The Prayer

### Fill

And the verb is *plēroō* — to fill full, to make complete. Not "give you some." Not "increase your joy."

The request is for saturation.

**Half-full is not what he prayed for.** So why is it what we settle for?

### All

And the adjective is total: *pasēs charas kai eirēnēs* — all joy and all peace. There is no category of joy left outside the request.

Which means the prayer assumes this is possible.

**Paul does not pray for what cannot be given.** Do you actually believe that is available?

### In Believing

And the sphere is faith. *En tō pisteuein* — in the act of believing, in the ongoing trust.

The filling happens inside the believing, not after it.

**Joy is not the reward for faith. It is the climate inside it.**

## Joy and Peace Together

### Why Both

And the pairing is deliberate. Joy without peace is excitement; peace without joy is resignation.

Together they are the interior of a person who is both delighted and settled.

**One is the music. The other is the floor.** And a floor makes music possible.

### Joy Is Forward

And joy looks at what is coming. It is the anticipation of a Person and a kingdom and a table.

Which is why it can coexist with a difficult week.

**Joy is not the absence of sorrow. It is the presence of something more.**

### Peace Is Downward

And peace is about what is underneath. The word *eirēnē* carries the Hebrew *shalom* — wholeness, nothing missing.

Peace is a foundation; joy is a weather.

**A person with peace can afford joy.**

### The Purpose Clause

### That Ye May Abound

And the reason is stated: *eis to perisseuein hymas en tē elpidi* — so that you may overflow in hope.

*Perisseuō* is the surplus word. To have more than enough.

**The filling is for the spilling.**

### The Chain

And notice the sequence. God of hope → fills with joy and peace → in believing → so you overflow in hope.

The hope comes out of the joy, and the joy comes out of the God of hope.

**You cannot give away a hope you have not been filled with.**

### Through the Power

And the agent is the Spirit. Not willpower, not positive thinking, not a good playlist.

The power of the Holy Ghost is the pump.

A reservoir needs a source, and the source is a Person.

### Why We Run Dry

### Because We Give Without Receiving

And the most common reason is simple depletion. People pour out for years and never refill.

The prayer assumes a rhythm.

An empty vessel cannot be generous.

### Because We Substitute Excitement

And the second is that we confuse stimulation with joy. A loud weekend is not a full tank; it is a temporary noise.

It empties faster than it fills.

**Excitement consumes. Joy supplies.**

### Because We Believe Partially

And the third is the phrase *in believing*. Where faith is thin, the filling has nothing to work in.

Doubt does not stop God's love, but it does stop the flow.

The pipe is your trust. The water is His.

### Staying Full

### Ask for the Filling

And the first practice is to pray this verse for yourself, out loud, with your own name in it. Paul prayed it for a church; you may pray it for a person.

A prayer in Scripture is already answered in principle.

### Guard the Inlet

And the second is to notice what actually fills you. Time in the Word, worship, honesty with a friend, rest, the sacraments of an ordinary meal.

Most people know their inlets. They simply do not schedule them.

**Nobody accidentally stays full.**

### Pour It Out

And the third is counter-intuitive: give it away. A reservoir with no outlet stagnates.

Hope shared is hope increased.

The overflow is the point, and the overflow requires somebody else in the room.

### The Boundary

Two guardrails.

First, this is not a promise of unbroken happiness. Paul wrote Romans 15 into a church with real divisions, and he himself knew sorrow.

Second, this is not a technique for manufacturing feelings. The filling is God's act; your part is believing and asking.

**The **The Life of Faith is where the filling happens; The Power of the Holy Spirit** is the source; and **The Kingdom of God is the joy that results.

## What This Means for You

1. Read Romans 15:1–13 in one sitting.

2. Pray verse 13 with your name in it.

3. Name your two best inlets and use them this week.

4. Give hope to one person who is running low.

**5. Keep faith and wisdom together.** Joy does not cancel wisdom. **The Life of Faith** is the trust; wisdom knows when to rest, when to ask for help, and when to say no to one more demand.

### Putting It to Work This Week

- **Read Ephesians 3:14–21.**
- **Read Psalm 4.**
- **Write the verse out and keep it visible.**
- **Do one thing that fills you.**
- **Do one thing that pours you out.**
