Month 9 has been one long argument, and it comes down to a preposition.

Not *in order to become* but *because you are*. Not "live right so that you may be righteous" but "live right because you are."

"Little children, let no man deceive you: he that doeth righteousness is righteous, even as he is righteous" (1 John 3:7).

Here is the central truth of this teaching: **the gospel never asks you to become righteous by acting right; it tells you that you are right and therefore to act like it — and the preposition is the whole difference between religion and rest.**

## The Verse's Careful Order

### Same Word, Two Uses

And the Greek makes the sentence almost musical: *ho poiōn tēn dikaiosynēn dikaios estin* — the one *doing* righteousness *is* righteous.

The doing and the being share a root. John is not separating them; he is ordering them.

**He does not say the doer becomes righteous. He says the doer is righteous.**

### The Standard Named

And the standard is startling: "even as he is righteous." As Christ is. Not "as well as he can manage."

That is not a bar to be climbed. It is a description of what was given.

**The measure of your righteousness is not your performance. It is His.**

### The Warning

And the verse opens with a warning — *let no man deceive you* — which means this specific point was already under attack when John wrote it.

**The oldest Christian confusion is not about who Jesus is. It is about the order.** Which order have you been living in?

## The Order Everywhere

### 1 Corinthians 6

And Paul uses the same structure with the Corinthian list of sins: "And such were some of you: but ye are washed, but ye are sanctified, but ye are justified" (1 Corinthians 6:11).

Past tense. Before any instruction about behaviour.

**He tells them what they were, then what they are, and only then what to do.**

### Ephesians 4–5

And Ephesians does it again: "put off concerning the former conversation the old man... and be renewed... and that ye put on the new man, which after God is created in righteousness" (Ephesians 4:22–24).

The new man is already *created*. The clothing is applied to something that exists.

**You cannot put on what has not been made. It has been made.**

### Colossians 3

And Colossians is bluntest of all: "If ye then be risen with Christ... For ye are dead, and your life is hid with Christ in God" (Colossians 3:1, 3).

Then, and only then, "mortify therefore your members" (Colossians 3:5).

**The imperative always rests on an indicative. Every command in the New Testament stands on a fact.**

### Why the Order Matters

### Because It Works

And the practical reason is that identity is the only engine strong enough to carry behaviour over time.

Rules change behaviour for a season. Identity changes it for good.

**You will eventually live like the person you believe you are.**

### Because It Is True

And the theological reason is simpler: the other order would be a lie. You cannot become what God has already declared you to be.

Trying to earn a verdict already given is not humility; it is unbelief dressed as devotion.

The order is not a technique for better living. It is the shape of the gospel.

### Because It Produces the Right Fruit

And the difference shows in the fruit. Behaviour managed by fear produces compliance; behaviour flowing from identity produces character.

The first can look impressive and feel like death.

God is not interested in a morality that could exist without Him.

### What "Live Right" Still Means

### Real Obedience

And none of this reduces obedience. John's letter is the most morally serious in the New Testament — he says the one who claims to know God and does not keep His commandments is a liar (1 John 2:4).

Grace never made a command optional.

The gospel does not lower the standard. It supplies the standing from which the standard can be met.

### Real Repentance

And when you fail, the response is not a collapse into identity crisis. It is the short path back: agree, receive, continue.

The identity did not change while you were failing.

A son who falls is still a son on the floor.

### Real Growth

And growth is real — the same John writes to little children, young men, and fathers (1 John 2:12–14). There are stages.

But the stages are in the walking, not in the standing.

You grow in the expression of sonship, never into it.

### Living It

### Begin With the Fact

And the daily practice is to begin with the fact before touching the to-do list.

Say what is true. Then do what follows.

### Act Your Age

And there is a holy irony in the phrase. Act like the son you are, the way a child in a good family grows into the family name.

Behaviour is the wardrobe of identity, not its price.

### Return Quickly

And when the two come apart — and they will — return to the fact immediately rather than working your way back to it.

The way back is always the way in: by grace, through faith.

### The Boundary

Two guardrails.

First, identity is not a substitute for obedience. "He that doeth righteousness" — the doing is in the verse.

Second, the fact is not a mood. On the days it feels unbelievable, it remains true, which is the whole reason it is called faith.

**The **Justification** is the verdict; **Righteousness is what you were given; and The New Creation is the person who now gets to live it out.

## What This Means for You

1. Rewrite your resolutions in the right order. "Because I am X, I will Y."

2. Read 1 Corinthians 6:9–11 and note the tense.

3. Name the one behaviour you keep failing at. What identity is it denying?

4. Say the fact out loud each morning before you do anything else.

**5. Keep faith and wisdom together.** Identity supplies the motive; wisdom builds the practice. **The New Creation** is who you are, and a settled plan is how a settled person lives it out in ordinary days.

### Putting It to Work This Week

- **Read 1 John 2:28–3:10 three times.**
- **Write five "because I am… I will…" statements.**
- **Read Colossians 3:1–14 and mark every imperative.**
- **Find the indicative under each one.**
- **Begin tomorrow with the fact, not the list.**
