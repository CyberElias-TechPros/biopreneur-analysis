Ephesians 3:19 asks for something so large that Paul immediately breaks into a doxology rather than finishing the sentence.

"And to know the love of Christ, which passeth knowledge, that ye might be filled with all the fulness of God."

Here is the central truth of this teaching: **Paul's request is not for a blessing but for a filling — and the thing that fills a believer is a love too large to be known, which means the goal of the Christian life is not to understand the love of Christ but to be submerged in it.**

## The Request

### Strengthened

And the prayer begins in the inner man, "that he would grant you, according to the riches of his glory, to be strengthened with might by his Spirit in the inner man" (Ephesians 3:16).

The inner man is the target. Not the circumstances.

**Paul prays for a stronger interior, not an easier exterior.** So which one have you been asking for?

### Rooted and Grounded

And the next image is botanical and architectural at once: rooted like a tree, grounded like a foundation (Ephesians 3:17).

Love is both the soil and the slab.

**You are not asked to build on love. You are asked to be built on it.** So what is your life actually sitting on?

### With All Saints

And there is a detail that changes the scale: this love is comprehended *with all saints* (Ephesians 3:18). Not privately.

The dimensions of the love of God require a body to measure them.

**Some things can only be understood together.**

## The Paradox

### To Know What Passes Knowledge

And Paul asks to know a love that surpasses knowledge. It is a deliberate contradiction.

He is not asking for information. He is asking for immersion.

**There is a knowing that is deeper than explanation.** And it is the only kind that changes anybody.

### Four Dimensions

And he names them: breadth, length, depth, height. Which is the language of a space you can be inside.

Not a fact to be believed. A room to live in.

**The love of Christ has floors and ceilings, and you are inside it.**

### The Fulness of God

And the destination of the prayer is *plērōthēte eis pan to plērōma tou Theou* — filled unto all the fulness of God, the same *plērōma* used of Christ in Colossians 2:9.

There is no higher request in the New Testament.

**Paul asks for the maximum, and asks it for ordinary believers.**

### Why Fullness Spills

### The Geometry

And the doxology tells you the intention: "Now unto him that is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think" (Ephesians 3:20).

*Hyperekperissou* — exceedingly beyond excess. The vocabulary of surplus.

**A full vessel under pressure always gives something away.**

### The River

And Jesus supplies the mechanism: "out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water" (John 7:38). Out, not in.

The filling has an outlet built into it.

A believer who is not overflowing is not yet full.

### The House

And the doxology closes with a doxology about glory in the church (Ephesians 3:21), which is the point of the whole prayer. A filled believer is not a private achievement; the fulness is for the house.

Which means the evidence of fulness is not how you feel on a Sunday. It is what your household looks like on a Monday.

A full vessel in a house changes the house, not just the vessel.

### The Body

And the fulness is described in Ephesians 1 as the church, which is "his body, the fulness of him that filleth all in all" (Ephesians 1:23).

The overflow is corporate. It fills a body, and the body fills a world.

You are not a reservoir with a lid. You are a pipe with a purpose. And a blocked pipe is not a full one; it is a stopped one.

### Living Loaded

### Ask Large

And the first practice is to pray this prayer, which is far bigger than most of our requests. Paul asked for the fulness of God in a letter about a church he had never visited.

Ask for the thing that would require God to be God.

A prayer you could answer yourself is too small.

### Stay Rooted

And the second is maintenance. Roots need soil and time; foundations need to be built before the storm.

The fulness is not a lightning strike. It is a life in a place.

You do not become full in a moment. You stay planted until it happens.

### Open the Outlet

And the third is direction. Find the people your overflow is meant for.

The surplus is never for storage.

God fills pipes, not cisterns.

### The Boundary

Two guardrails.

First, this is not a claim to deity. The fulness of God in a believer is a filling by grace, not an equality of nature.

Second, this is not a demand for constant euphoria. Paul's prayers are for depth, not for a permanently good mood.

**The **The Divine Nature is what is being filled; The Power of the Holy Spirit is the one doing it; and Divine Wholeness is the soundness that lets a full person carry the weight.

## What This Means for You

**1. Read Ephesians 3:14–21 slowly, on your knees if you can.**

**2. Pray it for yourself and for two others.**

3. Ask for one thing too big to answer alone.

**4. Name the outlet your overflow is meant for.**

**5. Keep faith and wisdom together.** Fulness does not remove limits. **The Divine Nature** is being filled; wisdom manages the vessel, the schedule, and the rest it needs.

### Putting It to Work This Week

- **Read Colossians 2:6–10.**
- **Read John 7:37–39.**
- **Write the doxology out and say it.**
- **Pray Paul's prayer for one person daily.**
- **Give one thing away** you were tempted to keep.
