Ephesians 6:11 has an unusual word in it, and it is the word that should reassure you most.

"Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil."

*Wiles* is *methodeia* — method, scheme, a calculated plan. It is the root of our word *method*.

Here is the central truth of this teaching: **the enemy's tactics are not creative because they do not need to be — he has been running the same three plays since Genesis 3, and the armour of God is the mature answer to each one.**

## The Original Scheme

### The Question

And Genesis 3 opens with a question that is not a question: "Yea, hath God said, Ye shall not eat of every tree of the garden?" (Genesis 3:1).

The serpent misquotes, exaggerates the prohibition, and invites Eve to inspect it. He does not deny God. He casts doubt on God's generosity.

**The first lie was not "there is no God." It was "God is holding out on you."**

### The Escalation

And then the flat contradiction: "Ye shall not surely die" (Genesis 3:4). Step one was doubt; step two is denial.

**First he questions the Word. Then he replaces it.**

### The Offer

And then the real bait: "ye shall be as gods, knowing good and evil" (Genesis 3:5).

The offer is autonomy. Not pleasure, not fruit — self-rule, the right to define good without reference to God.

**Every temptation since has been a variation on that sentence.**

## The Three Plays

### The Lust of the Flesh

And 1 John names the structure: "the lust of the flesh, and the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life" (1 John 2:16).

Appetite, acquisition, and status. That is the whole catalogue, and it has not been expanded in six thousand years.

**He is not imaginative. He is persistent.** Which of the three keeps finding you?

### The Method

And 2 Corinthians 2:11 warns against being ignorant of his *devices* — *noēmata*, thoughts, mental designs. His work is primarily cognitive.

**He does not usually come with a pitchfork. He comes with a plausible argument.**

**The battlefield is the space between an event and your interpretation of it.**

### Even the Saints

And this is why Paul feared for the Corinthians: "But I fear, lest by any means, as the serpent beguiled Eve through his subtilty, so your minds should be corrupted from the simplicity that is in Christ" (2 Corinthians 11:3).

The target is not the weak. It is the sincere.

**Corruption from simplicity. That is the method, and it does not need a scandal to work.**

### Why the Victory Is Older

### The Seed Promised

And the first Messianic promise in Scripture is spoken in the middle of the first temptation: "it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel" (Genesis 3:15).

Before the curse was announced, the cure was announced.

**The answer to the scheme was older than the scheme's first success.**

### The Disarming

And at the cross the tactics lost their teeth: "having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly" (Colossians 2:15).

The accuser can still accuse. He cannot condemn (Romans 8:34).

A prosecutor whose evidence has been dismissed can still talk. It changes nothing.

### The Limit Set

And Revelation's picture is a dragon on a chain (Revelation 20:2). The leash is the point.

Nothing reaches you without permission, and what is permitted is not permitted to destroy you.

The enemy has a method. He does not have the final word.

### Answering the Method

### Know the Play

And the practical answer is recognition. When doubt arrives about God's generosity, name it.

That is the same question from the garden — and it has the same answer.

A recognised scheme has already lost half its power.

### Answer With the Word

And Jesus' own model in the wilderness is instructive: three temptations, three quotations (Matthew 4:1–11). He did not argue; He answered.

Note also what the devil did — he quoted Scripture too, and misapplied it.

The Word in your hand, not just in your memory, is the weapon.

### Stand, Do Not Chase

And the armour's purpose is stated twice: *that ye may be able to stand* (Ephesians 6:11, 13). Not to advance, not to hunt.

Standing is the assignment.

The victory is Christ's. Your job is to hold ground.

### The Boundary

Two guardrails.

First, this is not a call to see a demon behind every problem. Most of the damage is done by the world and the flesh, and those need no help.

Second, this is not fear dressed in doctrine. A believer who is constantly hunting for the scheme has stopped trusting the covering.

**The **The Word of God is what every scheme distorts; the The Name of Jesus is what it must bow to; and Spiritual Warfare is standing rather than searching.

## What This Means for You

1. Learn the three plays. Flesh, eyes, pride.

2. Notice your favourite one. It is the one that works.

3. Write the answer verse for that specific scheme.

4. Stop arguing with the enemy. Answer and stand.

**5. Keep faith and wisdom together.** Discernment is a skill sharpened by Scripture and experience. **The Word of God** is the standard; wisdom is knowing when to speak and when to stand quietly.

### Putting It to Work This Week

- **Read Genesis 3:1–15 three times.**
- **Write out 1 John 2:16.**
- **Match each of the three plays to a recent temptation.**
- **Find the verse that answers yours.**
- **Read Matthew 4:1–11** and note how Jesus answered.
