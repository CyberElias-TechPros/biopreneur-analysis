Most arguments about the tithe are arguments about a percentage, and they miss the point of the whole practice.

Jesus put the matter somewhere else entirely: "For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also" (Matthew 6:21).

Notice the direction of that sentence, because it is the reverse of what we assume. We think the heart decides where the money goes. Jesus says the money decides where the heart goes.

Here is the central truth of this teaching: **the tithe is not a fee paid to God but a rehearsal that retrains the heart, because the money you release tells your heart what you actually believe about who is first.**

## What the Tenth Was

### Holy Before It Was Law

The tithe does not begin at Sinai. It appears in Genesis, centuries earlier, when Abraham met Melchizedek: "And he gave him tithes of all" (Genesis 14:20).

That matters for the argument. The tenth is older than the law, which is why the New Testament can discuss it in Hebrews without treating it as a abolished ordinance.

### The Lord's Portion

Leviticus states the principle with no ambiguity: "And all the tithe of the land, whether of the seed of the land, or of the fruit of the tree, is the LORD's: it is holy unto the LORD" (Leviticus 27:30).

Read the logic. The tenth is not a gift you decide to make. It is already *the LORD's*, and it is *holy* — set apart. The question the tithe asks is not "how much shall I give?" but "will I keep what is not mine?"

### First, Not Leftover

Proverbs gives the instruction in the form of a habit: "Honour the LORD with thy substance, and with the firstfruits of all thine increase: so shall thy barns be filled with plenty, and thy presses shall burst out with new wine" (Proverbs 3:9–10).

*Firstfruits* is the operative word. Not the remainder. Not what is left when the month has finished spending.

**The first thing given teaches the heart that God is not last.**

### The Rebuke in Malachi

Malachi 3 is the passage everyone quotes and few read carefully. The charge is specific: "Will a man rob God? Yet ye have robbed me. But ye say, Wherein have we robbed thee? In tithes and offerings" (Malachi 3:8).

Then the invitation, which is one of the only places in Scripture where God says "prove me": "Bring ye all the tithes into the storehouse... and prove me now herewith, saith the LORD of hosts, if I will not open you the windows of heaven" (Malachi 3:10).

**The issue in Malachi was not poverty. It was a people who had decided God could have the leftovers.**

### What It Trains

#### Priority

Every budget is a creed. It states, in numbers, what you actually believe about the future, about security, and about God.

A tenth placed first says: He is before the rent, before the savings, before the emergency fund. **That sentence, repeated monthly, changes what a person believes.**

#### Trust

The tithe is a small, regular exercise in trusting God with the arithmetic.

It is one thing to say God is your provider. It is another to move the first tenth out of the account on the first day and watch the month still work.

Faith is not built by resolving to trust. It is built by repeatedly doing something that requires it.

#### Detachment

Here is the part almost nobody says: the tithe teaches your hand to open.

Most people's financial anxiety comes from a grip that cannot loosen. A tenth given every month, in good months and bad, trains a nervous system to survive the releasing.

A person who can give away ten per cent is a person who is not owned by ninety.

#### Honour

Proverbs ties the practice to honour rather than obligation: "Honour the LORD with thy substance" (Proverbs 3:9).

The Hebrew for honour, *kābēd*, is related to weight. To honour is to treat someone as heavy — as significant, as carrying weight in your decisions. **The tithe is how you give God weight in your finances.**

#### The Boundary

Two honest guardrails, because this subject is often abused.

First, generosity in the New Testament is never coerced. Paul is explicit: "not grudgingly, or of necessity" (2 Corinthians 9:7). Any teaching that threatens you into giving has departed from the Spirit of the thing.

Second, the tithe is not a transaction that obligates God. It is not a vending machine, and treating it as one turns worship into commerce. **Faith and Wisdom** hold the practice as a habit of the heart, not a formula for returns.

## What This Means for You

**1. Decide the order, not the amount first.** Put the first portion aside before anything else in the month.

**2. Give it to a place that is actually doing the work.** The storehouse in Malachi was the place where the ministry was fed.

**3. Do it in the lean months too.** The rehearsal is most formative when it is most costly.

**4. Let the heart catch up.** If it feels like a loss, do it anyway, and watch what happens to the feeling over a year.

**5. Keep faith and wisdom together.** Tithe and budget. Generosity and provision for your household. **Kingdom Stewardship** does both, and Scripture never sets them against each other.

## Putting It to Work This Week

- **Read Matthew 6:19–24** and mark every mention of treasure.
- **Read Proverbs 3:9–10** and write out what it promises.
- **Read Malachi 3:6–12** slowly, and note who is speaking.
- **Look at last month's spending** and work out what it says you believe.
- **Set the first portion aside** before anything else this month.
