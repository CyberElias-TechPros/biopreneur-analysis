Second Samuel 6 tells the same story twice, and the differences between the two tellings are the whole of this teaching.

"And David danced before the LORD with all his might; and David was girded with a linen ephod" (2 Samuel 6:14).

Here is the central truth of this teaching: **the presence of God is not carried by effort but by everything — and Scripture tells two stories of the same journey to show that the difference between an ox cart and a shoulder is the difference between a sensation and a stewardship.**

## The First Attempt

### A New Cart

And the first time David moved the ark, he used a new cart (2 Samuel 6:3). It was modern, it was expensive, and it was how the Philistines had done it (1 Samuel 6:7).

The form was borrowed from people who had never carried it.

**An old method can look new and still be a borrowed cart.**

### Uzzah's Hand

And the oxen shook it, and Uzzah put out his hand to steady it, and he died (2 Samuel 6:7).

That is a hard verse, and it deserves a fair reading. Uzzah was not contemptuous; he was protective. But the ark was never in danger of falling. The God who rode above the mercy seat did not need a human hand to keep His own throne upright.

**The presence does not need your protection. It needs your obedience.**

### The Number That Stopped It

And David was offended: "How shall the ark of the LORD come to me?" (2 Samuel 6:9).

The question behind the question is the right one. If this is what happens, who can be near Him?

**Awe that stops the journey has not yet become understanding.**

## The Second Attempt

### On Shoulders

And three months later David read the law, and this time the ark travelled on the shoulders of the Levites, as Moses had commanded (1 Chronicles 15:15).

Same ark, same destination, same enthusiasm. Different method.

**The honour of the presence is measurable in the way you handle it.**

### The Method Was in the Book

And the correction did not come from a new revelation. It came from the passage that had been there the entire time: Numbers 4:15.

The cart was not wrong because it was modern. It was wrong because the instruction had said shoulders.

**Most of our most painful mistakes are not mysteries. They are instructions we did not read.**

### The Dancing

And the second telling has David dancing. Which means the reverence and the joy are not opposites, and never were. The first attempt had reverence and no obedience; the second had both, and the joy was possible because the handling was right.

**The people who handle His presence correctly are the ones who can celebrate freely.**

### What Carrying Means Now

### You Are the Carrier

And the application is not about furniture. Paul's argument is direct: "your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you" (1 Corinthians 6:19).

The ark was a box with a seat on it. The believer is a person with a Person in them.

**The carrying is not an office you visit. It is a body you live in.**

### Priests, Not Porters

And the Levites were set apart for exactly this. They had no inheritance in the land because He was their inheritance (Numbers 18:20).

Which is why the church's great distinction is not its buildings.

The greatest thing you will ever carry is not a message you can hand to someone else.

### Handled With Understanding

And the practical content of "honour" is not gloom. It is attention: the things you do with the body, the tone in your house, the words you repeat, the way you speak about Him when He is not the subject.

Reverence is a tone of life, not a volume of music.

### The Cost and the Joy

### Michal's Contempt

And there is one more figure in the story, and she is the warning. Michal watched from a window and despised him in her heart (2 Samuel 6:16), and she ended barren.

It is possible to be close to the presence and be outside the joy.

Criticism from the window is not holiness. It is distance with a good view.

### Obed-Edom's House

And during the three months the ark rested there, the man's entire household was blessed (2 Samuel 6:11).

Which means the presence is a net gain wherever it actually rests, even when the journey is paused.

God's presence blesses the house it is welcomed into, not merely the heart that claims it.

### The Boundary

Two guardrails.

First, this is not a warrant for carelessness with the Spirit's feelings. He is described as being grieved (Ephesians 4:30) and as a Person, not a power current.

Second, this is not a call to morbid caution. The same ark that killed Uzzah blessed Obed-Edom. The presence is not dangerous — mishandling is.

**The **The Presence of God** is the treasure; **Communion With the Holy Spirit is the relationship that teaches you how to carry it; and The Anointing is what rests on a life handled rightly.

## What This Means for You

1. Read both accounts: 2 Samuel 6 and 1 Chronicles 15.

2. Ask what your ox cart is — where have you followed a borrowed method?

3. Read the instruction before you move.

**4. Handle your body this week like a dwelling.**

**5. Keep faith and wisdom together.** Carrying Him does not mean informality in every room. **The Presence of God** is not a managed resource; wisdom knows which rooms a carrier should walk into, and how.

### Putting It to Work This Week

- **Read Numbers 4:15 and 1 Chronicles 15:12–15.**
- **Read 1 Corinthians 6:12–20.**
- **Write down one borrowed method.**
- **Change one habit of handling.**
- **Dance about it** when you get it right.
