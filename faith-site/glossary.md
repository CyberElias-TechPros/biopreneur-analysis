*36 core conceptual terms across the 12 doctrinal families · v1.0*

## Usage Rules (Terminity Law)

1. **First-use definition:** Every article highlights **1–3 glossary terms** and defines each plainly in the sentence or paragraph where it first appears. Never assume a reader already knows church shorthand.
2. **Capitalization rule:** Capitalize a term when naming it as a formal biblical doctrine or title (e.g., *the New Birth*, *the Name of Jesus*, *the Finished Work*); keep it lowercase in ordinary descriptive prose (e.g., *you have a new nature*, *walk in spiritual understanding*).
3. **Meaning stability:** Once defined, a term means the same thing in Day 001 and Day 365. Do not invent one-off buzzwords that fight this glossary.
4. **Contrast boundary:** Every term below carries a clear boundary (*what it is* vs. *what it is not*) so conviction never drifts into presumption or legalism.

## I. New Creation, Identity & Redemption (9 terms)

### 1. The New Birth
- **Definition:** The spiritual regeneration wrought by the Holy Spirit when a person believes in Jesus Christ, imparting God's own life to the human spirit.
- **Anchors:** John 3:3–7 · 1 Peter 1:23 · Titus 3:5
- **Boundary:** Regeneration of the human spirit — *not* turning over a moral leaf or joining a denomination.
- **Category:** New Creation & Identity

### 2. The New Creation
- **Definition:** The believer in Christ as a brand-new kind (*kainos*) of spiritual being whose old Adamic standing has ended and whose inward nature is created after God.
- **Anchors:** 2 Corinthians 5:17 · Galatians 6:15 · Ephesians 4:24
- **Boundary:** Present spiritual identity — *not* a future upgrade earned by self-improvement.
- **Category:** New Creation & Identity

### 3. Divine Life (*Zoe*)
- **Definition:** The uncreated, eternal life and nature of God Himself, imparted to the believer's spirit through union with Jesus Christ.
- **Anchors:** John 5:26 · John 10:10 · 1 John 5:11–12
- **Boundary:** Spiritual life shared with Christ — *not* mere biological existence (*bios*) or emotional excitement (*psuche*).
- **Category:** New Creation & Identity

### 4. The Divine Nature
- **Definition:** God's moral and spiritual character planted as seed within the born-again spirit, enabling the believer to love righteousness and outgrow corruption through the Word.
- **Anchors:** 2 Peter 1:3–4 · 1 John 3:9 · Ephesians 4:24
- **Boundary:** Participatory likeness in Christ — *never* a claim that a human becomes deity or independent of God.
- **Category:** New Creation & Identity

### 5. Sonship
- **Definition:** The full legal adoption and family relationship of every believer as a born child and heir of God through Christ, marked by intimacy ("Abba, Father") rather than slavish fear.
- **Anchors:** Romans 8:14–17 · Galatians 4:4–7 · 1 John 3:1–2
- **Boundary:** Shared family standing for every believer (male and female) in Christ — *not* an elite tier of Christians.
- **Category:** New Creation & Identity

### 6. The Inheritance of the Believer
- **Definition:** Everything Christ secured by His death and resurrection and shares with the saints in light — forgiveness, righteousness, the Spirit, peace, wisdom, and eternal glory.
- **Anchors:** Colossians 1:12 · Ephesians 1:11–14 · Acts 20:32
- **Boundary:** Covenantal provision received by faith — *not* greed-driven entitlement.
- **Category:** New Creation & Identity

### 7. The Finished Work
- **Definition:** The complete, once-for-all redemptive accomplishment of Jesus Christ in His sinless life, substitutionary cross, burial, resurrection, and enthronement.
- **Anchors:** John 19:30 · Hebrews 10:12–14 · Romans 4:25
- **Boundary:** A completed atonement to rest in and live from — *not* an unfinished transaction awaiting human penance.
- **Category:** Redemption & His Return

### 8. The Blood of Jesus
- **Definition:** Christ's sacrificial life laid down on the cross, establishing the eternal New Covenant, cleansing the conscience, and securing permanent remission of sins.
- **Anchors:** Hebrews 9:12–14 · Ephesians 1:7 · 1 Peter 1:18–19
- **Boundary:** Objective covenantal cleansing — *not* a superstitious charm repeated out of fear.
- **Category:** Redemption & His Return

### 9. The Ministry of Reconciliation
- **Definition:** The stewardship entrusted to every new-creation believer to announce that God was in Christ reconciling the world to Himself, inviting people home to God.
- **Anchors:** 2 Corinthians 5:18–20 · Romans 5:10–11
- **Boundary:** Grace-filled ambassadorship — *not* harsh condemnation of the lost.
- **Category:** Redemption & His Return

## II. Righteousness, Justification & Grace (5 terms)

### 10. Righteousness
- **Definition:** Rightness with God — both the legal gift of right standing imputed to the believer in Christ and the new nature created within the reborn spirit that produces right living.
- **Anchors:** 2 Corinthians 5:21 · Romans 3:21–26 · Romans 5:17
- **Boundary:** A gift received by faith that bears ethical fruit — *neither* human merit *nor* license to sin.
- **Category:** Righteousness & Grace

### 11. The Consciousness of Righteousness
- **Definition:** The settled inward awareness that one stands forgiven, accepted, and clean before the Father in Christ, freeing the believer from guilt-driven religion.
- **Anchors:** Hebrews 10:1–2, 19–22 · Romans 8:1 · 1 Corinthians 15:34
- **Boundary:** Humble confidence in Christ's blood — *not* moral arrogance or denial when correction is needed.
- **Category:** Righteousness & Grace

### 12. Justification
- **Definition:** God's supreme courtroom verdict declaring the believing sinner wholly righteous and acquitted on the basis of Christ's obedience and blood.
- **Anchors:** Romans 3:24 · Romans 5:1 · Romans 8:33–34
- **Boundary:** Once-for-all legal acquittal — *distinct from* progressive daily maturity (sanctification in conduct).
- **Category:** Righteousness & Grace

### 13. Legal Position
- **Definition:** Who and where the believer is before God's throne *in Christ* — seated with Him, accepted in the Beloved, and complete in Him — prior to fluctuating feelings.
- **Anchors:** Ephesians 1:6 · Ephesians 2:6 · Colossians 2:10
- **Boundary:** Covenant standing in Christ — *never* an excuse to ignore practical obedience in daily conduct.
- **Category:** Righteousness & Grace

### 14. The Glorious Liberty of the Children of God
- **Definition:** The spiritual freedom from condemnation, fear of death, and bondage to sin enjoyed now in the Spirit and destined to be manifested fully at the redemption of the body.
- **Anchors:** Romans 8:21 · Galatians 5:1, 13 · 2 Corinthians 3:17
- **Boundary:** Freedom to love and serve by the Spirit — *never* indulgence of the flesh.
- **Category:** Righteousness & Grace

## III. The Word, Revelation & Mind Renewal (6 terms)

### 15. The Word of God
- **Definition:** God's authoritative self-revelation — supremely in the person of Jesus the living Word (*Logos*) and written in the inspired Scriptures, carrying God's own creative power.
- **Anchors:** John 1:1, 14 · 2 Timothy 3:16–17 · Hebrews 4:12
- **Boundary:** The final rule of faith and doctrine — *never* subordinated to private impressions or cultural trends.
- **Category:** The Word

### 16. Spiritual Understanding
- **Definition:** Spirit-illuminated comprehension of how God's Word fits together and applies to real life, moving truth from memorized text into governing wisdom.
- **Anchors:** Colossians 1:9–10 · Ephesians 1:17–18 · Luke 24:45
- **Boundary:** Scripture-grounded insight — *not* esoteric secret knowledge detached from the biblical text.
- **Category:** The Word

### 17. Revelation Knowledge
- **Definition:** The unveiling of truth already written in Scripture as the Holy Spirit opens the eyes of the heart to see Christ and our life in Him clearly.
- **Anchors:** Ephesians 1:17–19 · 1 Corinthians 2:9–12 · Matthew 16:17
- **Boundary:** Illumination of the written Word — *never* new canonical scripture competing with the Bible.
- **Category:** The Word

### 18. Spiritual Realities
- **Definition:** Unseen covenantal facts established by God's Word — such as Christ's lordship, the indwelling Spirit, and the new creation — which endure longer than temporary circumstances.
- **Anchors:** 2 Corinthians 4:18 · Hebrews 11:1, 3 · Ephesians 1:3
- **Boundary:** Scriptural truth held in sober faith — *not* pretending physical facts do not exist.
- **Category:** The Unseen Realm / Spiritual Warfare

### 19. Renewing of the Mind
- **Definition:** The ongoing lifelong process of replacing old, fearful, flesh-shaped habits of thought with God's way of thinking through steady meditation and practice of Scripture.
- **Anchors:** Romans 12:2 · Ephesians 4:23 · Philippians 4:8
- **Boundary:** Deliberate scriptural retraining of thought and attitude — *unlike* the instantaneous New Birth of the spirit.
- **Category:** Christian Living

### 20. Meditation in the Word
- **Definition:** Slow, attentive pondering, muttering, and turning over of Scripture until its meaning saturates thought, speech, and decision-making.
- **Anchors:** Joshua 1:8 · Psalm 1:2–3 · 1 Timothy 4:15
- **Boundary:** Filling the mind with Scripture — *opposite* to emptying the mind in Eastern mysticism.
- **Category:** The Word

## IV. The Holy Spirit & Communion (5 terms)

### 21. The Life of the Spirit
- **Definition:** Daily Christian living energized, guided, and fruited by the indwelling Holy Spirit rather than willpower or external rule-keeping alone.
- **Anchors:** Romans 8:2–11 · Galatians 5:16, 22–25
- **Boundary:** Fruitful holiness and love — *not* disorder or impulsiveness blamed on the Spirit.
- **Category:** The Holy Spirit

### 22. The Power of the Holy Spirit
- **Definition:** The dynamic enabling (*dunamis*) of God's Spirit resting upon and working within believers for bold witness, Christlike endurance, and ministry to others.
- **Anchors:** Acts 1:8 · Romans 15:13 · Ephesians 3:16, 20
- **Boundary:** Christ-exalting service — *not* self-promotion or theatrical display.
- **Category:** The Holy Spirit

### 23. The Anointing
- **Definition:** The abiding presence and consecrating empowerment of the Holy Spirit in and upon the believer through Christ the Anointed One, teaching truth and lifting burdens.
- **Anchors:** 1 John 2:20, 27 · Luke 4:18 · 2 Corinthians 1:21
- **Boundary:** Shared gift in Christ — *not* a personality cult around human titles.
- **Category:** The Holy Spirit

### 24. The Presence of God
- **Definition:** Both God's faithful covenantal nearness dwelling continually in the believer as His temple and His manifest fellowship experienced in worship, prayer, and obedience.
- **Anchors:** 1 Corinthians 3:16 · Hebrews 13:5 · Psalm 16:11
- **Boundary:** Settled promise ("I will never leave thee") — *not* dependent on goosebumps or mood.
- **Category:** Prayer & Fasting

### 25. Communion With the Holy Spirit
- **Definition:** Ongoing personal fellowship, partnership, sensitivity, and conversation with the Holy Spirit throughout ordinary work and prayer.
- **Anchors:** 2 Corinthians 13:14 · Philippians 2:1 · John 14:16–17
- **Boundary:** Relational attentiveness to a Divine Person — *never* treating the Spirit as an impersonal force.
- **Category:** The Holy Spirit

## V. Faith, Speech & Authority (6 terms)

### 26. The Life of Faith
- **Definition:** Trusting God's character and acting on His Word as reliable truth in every season, whether feelings and circumstances immediately agree or not.
- **Anchors:** Habakkuk 2:4 · Romans 1:17 · 2 Corinthians 5:7 · Hebrews 11:1, 6
- **Boundary:** Trust in God Himself — *never* mechanical willpower or denial of wise responsibility.
- **Category:** Faith

### 27. Confession of the Word (*Homologia*)
- **Definition:** Speaking the same thing God says in Scripture — aligning the mouth with the heart's faith in Christ's person and promises.
- **Anchors:** Romans 10:9–10 · Hebrews 4:14 · Hebrews 10:23 · 2 Corinthians 4:13
- **Boundary:** Honest agreement with Scripture — *not* magic incantations or outcome guarantees detached from God's wisdom.
- **Category:** Faith

### 28. The Name of Jesus
- **Definition:** The revealed person, accomplished redemptive work, and supreme sovereign authority of the risen Lord Jesus Christ, in whom believers pray and minister.
- **Anchors:** Philippians 2:9–11 · John 16:23–24 · Acts 3:16 · Colossians 3:17
- **Boundary:** Authorized representation under Christ's lordship — *not* a verbal formula used apart from submission to Him.
- **Category:** Authority of the Believer

### 29. The Authority of the Believer
- **Definition:** The delegated right (*exousia*) given by Christ to His church to stand against spiritual darkness, proclaim the gospel, and pray in His Name.
- **Anchors:** Luke 10:19 · Matthew 28:18–20 · Ephesians 1:19–23; 6:10–18
- **Boundary:** Spiritual authority over the enemy's lies and temptations — *never* domination over other human beings.
- **Category:** Authority of the Believer

### 30. The Kingdom of God
- **Definition:** God's sovereign saving reign in Christ — already present within believers in righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Ghost, and coming in visible fullness at Christ's return.
- **Anchors:** Romans 14:17 · Colossians 1:13 · Luke 17:20–21 · Revelation 11:15
- **Boundary:** "Already and not yet" reign of Christ — *not* partisan political nationalism.
- **Category:** Authority of the Believer

### 31. The Supernatural Life
- **Definition:** Ordinary human responsibilities — marriage, work, generosity, integrity, prayer — lived from the indwelling grace and wisdom of God rather than mere human strength.
- **Anchors:** Galatians 2:20 · Colossians 1:29 · Philippians 4:13
- **Boundary:** Grace empowering everyday faithfulness — *not* escapism from earthly duties.
- **Category:** Christian Living

## VI. Practical Walk, Wholeness & Stewardship (5 terms)

### 32. Faith and Wisdom
- **Definition:** The biblical partnership between wholehearted trust in God's promises and responsible practical action — pairing prayer with diligence, confession with integrity, and faith for healing with sound medical care.
- **Anchors:** Proverbs 4:7 · James 2:17 · Luke 14:28 · Nehemiah 4:9
- **Boundary:** Protects readers from fatalism on one side and reckless presumption on the other.
- **Category:** Christian Living

### 33. Divine Wholeness
- **Definition:** God's redemptive concern for the whole person — spirit, soul, and body — grounded in Christ's compassion and atonement, walked out now in faith, peace, and wise stewardship of health, and consummated at the resurrection.
- **Anchors:** 1 Thessalonians 5:23 · Isaiah 53:4–5 · Matthew 8:16–17 · 3 John 2 · Romans 8:23
- **Boundary:** Hopeful, grateful trust in God the Healer that honors doctors and never shames those walking through illness.
- **Category:** Divine Health

### 34. Kingdom Stewardship
- **Definition:** Managing time, skill, work, and money as a faithful trustee of God's generosity — working diligently, living debt-wise, providing for household needs, and giving cheerfully.
- **Anchors:** 2 Corinthians 9:6–11 · 1 Corinthians 4:2 · Ephesians 4:28 · 1 Timothy 6:17–19
- **Boundary:** Generous, diligent stewardship — *never* transactional "give-to-get-rich" schemes or guaranteed financial returns.
- **Category:** Prosperity & Stewardship

### 35. Spiritual Warfare
- **Definition:** Standing firm in the armor of God and the truth of Scripture to demolish deceptive arguments, resist temptation, and hold the ground Christ has already won.
- **Anchors:** Ephesians 6:10–18 · 2 Corinthians 10:3–5 · Colossians 2:15 · James 4:7
- **Boundary:** Directed strictly against spiritual deception ("wrestle not against flesh and blood") — *never* demonizing people, neighbors, or denominations.
- **Category:** Spiritual Warfare

### 36. The Blessed Hope
- **Definition:** The joyful, purifying expectation of the personal, visible, glorious return of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ to raise the dead in Christ and make all things new.
- **Anchors:** Titus 2:11–14 · 1 Thessalonians 4:13–18 · 1 John 3:2–3 · Revelation 21:1–5
- **Boundary:** Awakens holy, purposeful living today — *never* date-setting or passive withdrawal.
- **Category:** Redemption & His Return
